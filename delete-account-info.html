NicTrack - Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your NicTrack account and the personal data associated with it.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the NicTrack app on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Manage Subscription (if applicable) and cancel any active subscription first through the App Store or Google Play.
  4. Return to Profile, scroll down, and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion. Your account and associated data will be removed immediately.

If you cannot access the app or need help, email us at niko.tadic1804@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line and we will process it within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your profile (email, display name, avatar, referral code)
  • Nicotine usage logs and statistics
  • Buddy connections and buddy messages
  • Craving logs and notes
  • Device/push-notification tokens
  • Streak and milestone records

What data may be kept

Some data must be retained for legal, security, or technical reasons:
  • Purchase records: Transaction records handled by Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat may be retained in accordance with their policies and legal/tax requirements. We do not store full payment information.
  • Aggregated/anonymized analytics: Data that has been stripped of personally identifiable information may be retained for product improvement.
  • Backups: Secure backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days before being fully purged.

Data deletion without account deletion

NicTrack currently requires deleting your full account to remove personal data. There is no separate "delete some data while keeping the account" feature.
